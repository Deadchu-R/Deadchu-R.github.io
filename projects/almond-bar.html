Almond Bar

Unreal Engine quest game

My hometown bar, rebuilt brighter. Find the key, talk to the regulars, finish the quest.

The final project for my Unreal Engine Blueprints course: an adventure game set in a bar, where the player must retrieve a key and complete a quest.

Inspired by my hometown bar, CharlieBar, I blended its atmosphere with a modern, brightly lit design.
