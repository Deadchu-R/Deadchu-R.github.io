Boss Rush

Grid-based tactical combat

Three heroes, one giant boss, and a grid. Medicines against disease, inside a microscopic world.

Developed in my final year for the Game Production 2 course: a grid-based strategy game where the player controls a party of three characters to defeat a powerful boss.

Set in a microscopic world, it pits bacteria and diseases against medicines, with a focus on strategic teamwork and coordination.

What I did

  • Designed the core gameplay loop, the tactical grid mechanics and the overall balance
  • Led a cross-functional team of programmers and artists for nine months, through delivery
  • Resolved conflicts and blockers between team members to keep the workflow smooth
