Privacy

KesherAI privacy policy

KesherAI is free, open-source software. It collects no data and sends nothing to its makers.

The app

The app runs on your own computer. Chats, files and models stay there. It talks to an online service only when you connect one yourself (for example a cloud model provider, Google Drive or Telegram), and then only to do what you asked.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, KesherAI uses your account only on your own devices. If you turn on "Tasks on all your devices", KesherAI keeps your tasks in its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It cannot see any of your other Drive files. The tasks are locked (encrypted) before they are saved, and only your own devices read them. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. KesherAI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is never sold, shared, or used for ads.

The Kesher Cloud hub

The optional hub (a personal page each user runs for themselves) only passes sealed messages between your own devices and keeps no tasks or files. It knows each device's name, public key and when it was last seen.

The browser extension

It reads only the tabs and pages you ask KesherAI to work with, sends them only to the KesherAI app on your own computer (127.0.0.1), and keeps only its connection key. It never types into password or payment fields, and never sells or shares data.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/Deadchu-R/KesherAI/issues.
