Fruits and Vegetables

This section showcases my knowledge and passion for fruits and vegetables.

Fruits

  • Apples - Rich in fiber and vitamin C
  • Bananas - Great source of potassium
  • Strawberries - High in antioxidants
  • Oranges - Excellent vitamin C content

Vegetables

  • Broccoli - Packed with vitamins K and C
  • Carrots - Rich in beta-carotene
  • Spinach - High in iron and folate
  • Tomatoes - Good source of lycopene
